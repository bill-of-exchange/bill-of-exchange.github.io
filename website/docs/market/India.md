# India

India's law of negotiable instruments, the Negotiable Instruments Act 1881, is a year older than the UK Act ours is drawn under and belongs to the same family. Its electronic infrastructure for bills of exchange is the receivables-discounting system licensed by the central bank; a general law on electronic trade documents was published for comment in 2026.

## Law 

[Reserve Bank of India (Trade Receivables Discounting System) Directions, 2026](https://www.rxil.in/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/RBI-TReDS-Guidelines-2026.pdf) — Sellers upload "invoices or bills of exchange" as factoring units; platforms must run bidding among financiers and record assignments with CERSAI ([Vinod Kothari](https://vinodkothari.com/2026/06/treds-master-directions-issued-by-rbi/)).

[Digital Trade Facilitation Bill 2026](https://tradetreasurypayments.com/articles/india-invites-comments-on-the-digital-trade-facilitation-bill-2026-to-promote-electronic-trade-documents-recognition) — Draft law published for public comment to recognise electronic trade documents; whether it amends the Negotiable Instruments Act has not been verified.

## Systems and projects

[Trade Receivables Discounting System (TReDS)](https://website.rbi.org.in/web/rbi/-/statistics/entity-wise-trade-receivables-discounting-system-treds-statistics-for-the-month-of-december-2025) — Electronic platforms authorised by the Reserve Bank of India (RXIL, M1xchange, Invoicemart and newer entrants) on which small and medium enterprises sell receivables, including bills of exchange, to competing financiers after acceptance by the buyer. M1xchange passed INR 1 lakh crore (about US$11 billion) of throughput in the 2025–26 financial year ([BCR](https://bcrpub.com/news/m1xchange-crosses-inr-1-lakh-crore-us11-04bn-throughput-in-fy25-26/)).

**Compared with our bills:** the buyer's acceptance on the platform plays the role our drawee's acceptance plays on chain, which makes TReDS the closest Indian analogue of our acceptance ledger. After acceptance the two part ways: a TReDS unit is sold once to a financier and settled at maturity inside a licensed platform among identified parties; our accepted bill passes from bearer to bearer on a public register and is paid at sight. TReDS digitises the financing of a bill; we digitise the bill.
