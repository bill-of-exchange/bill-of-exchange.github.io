# Latin America

Latin America has the most mature live electronic credit instruments outside East Asia: Argentina's electronic cheque now carries most of the cheque clearing by value, and Brazil, Colombia and Peru run national registries of electronic trade bills. All are central registries reached through banks or licensed registrars.

## Argentina

[BCRA: ECHEQ, the electronic cheque](https://www.bcra.gob.ar/en/echeq-or-electronic-check/) — A cheque "that can be drawn, endorsed, guaranteed, negotiated" electronically, under the same rules as paper cheques except where specific rules apply; issued and deposited only through financial institutions, with unlimited indorsements, and stored in a system administered by the clearing house COELSA. Regulated by BCRA Communication "A" 6578 ([text](https://www.consejosalta.org.ar/wp-content/uploads/BCRA-6578.pdf)). In October 2025 ECHEQ carried 58.9% of cleared cheques by number and 81.8% by value; dollar cheques are electronic only, and the central bank intends to end paper cheques ([iProfesional](https://www.iprofesional.com/finanzas/442044-el-bcra-avanza-hacia-el-fin-del-cheque-en-papel-como-sera-el-sistema-solo-con-e-cheq)).

**Compared with our bills:** ECHEQ is the live system closest to our purpose: a negotiable payment instrument in native electronic form, at national scale. It remains a cheque drawn on a bank account, payable to a named person, indorsed inside a central repository and cleared in 48 hours. Our bill is accepted by its drawee before it circulates, needs no account at the holder's end, passes to bearer by a transfer that is final when the block is confirmed, and is kept on a register that no clearing house administers.

## Brazil

[Duplicata escritural](https://b3.com.br/pt_br/noticias/duplicata-escritural.htm) — Book-entry form of the duplicata, the Brazilian trade bill, under Lei 13.775/2018 and Central Bank of Brazil resolutions, recorded by authorised registrars such as B3 and Núclea; supervised production began in July 2026 and registration becomes mandatory in stages from June 2027 to June 2028 ([Santander](https://www.santander.com.br/blog/duplicata-escritural)).

**Compared with our bills:** a trade instrument tied to an invoice and registered by licensed registrars so that it can be pledged or sold. Our bill is not tied to any transaction and its register is public.

## Mexico

[Reform of the General Law of Negotiable Instruments and Credit Operations (LGTOC), March 2024](https://www.mondaq.com/mexico/financial-services/1451512/mexican-reform-regarding-electronic-negotiable-instruments) — Allows promissory notes, cheques and other instruments to be issued, transferred and indorsed as data messages. Courts have since required an advanced electronic signature from a certification provider and record-keeping under the NOM-151 standard for an electronic pagaré ([CCN Law](https://ccn-law.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/PDF20251202_CCN_emision-y-endoso-de-pagares-digitales-3.pdf)).

**Compared with our bills:** the most technology-neutral reform in the region on its face; in practice the courts have added certified signatures and certified archiving, which the signature of a blockchain transaction does not provide. English law prescribes no form of signature, which is why our transaction signature suffices there.

## Colombia

[DIAN: RADIAN](https://micrositios.dian.gov.co/sistema-de-facturacion-electronica/abece-radian/) — Registry of the tax authority for electronic sales invoices that circulate as negotiable instruments (títulos valor), recording acceptance, indorsement, guarantees and payment events.

**Compared with our bills:** the state registers each event in the life of the invoice; our contract registers acceptance and transfer in a public ledger that the parties write to themselves.

## Peru

[Factura negociable at CAVALI](https://forbes.pe/economia-y-finanzas/2025-02-07/el-volumen-de-facturas-negociables-en-peru-crecio-un-13-en-2024-y-supero-los-s-43-000-millones) — Negotiable invoices registered at the central securities depository CAVALI; about S/43 billion negotiated in 2024 with nearly 50,000 suppliers.

**Compared with our bills:** an invoice-based credit instrument registered at a depository and sold to financiers; our bill is a payment instrument held by its bearer.
