# Israel 

Israel has digitised the clearing of cheques, not the cheque itself. The Bank of Israel has stated what a digital substitute for the paper cheque should do; no such instrument is in use yet.

## Bank of Israel 

[Bank of Israel: Digital Shekel: Questions and Answers](http://boi.org.il/en/questions-and-answers/digital-shekel/) 

[Bank of Israel: Digital shekel project – Technological experiment on a distributed platform](https://boi.org.il/en/communication-and-publications/press-releases/digital-shekel-project-technological-experiment-on-a-distributed-platform/) 


**Compared with our bills:** a central bank digital currency is the liability of the central bank. An accepted bill is the liability of its acceptor, a bank or another institution; it needs no new money, only the existing law of bills of exchange and an acceptor willing to sign.

## Cheques

[Electronic Check Clearing Law 5776-2016](https://www.boi.org.il/en/communication-and-publications/press-releases/publication-of-the-electronic-check-clearing-law-5776-2016/) (חוק סליקה אלקטרונית של שיקים, התשע"ו-2016) — Allows cheques to be cleared as images; the paper cheque remains the instrument. Since 2 September 2026 post-dated cheques can be deposited through the banks' mobile applications ([Bank of Israel](https://boi.org.il/en/communication-and-publications/press-releases/the-bank-of-israel-announces-that-effective-september-2-2026-post-dated-checks-can-be-deposited-using-the-banks-cellular-applications/)).

[Bank of Israel: public consultation on a digital substitute for paper checks (2022)](https://www.boi.org.il/en/communication-and-publications/press-releases/extension-of-the-public-consultation-on-the-technological-specification-of-a-digital-substitute-for-paper-checks/) — Consultation on the technological specification of "digital debit instructions" to replace paper cheques. The stated aims: keep the credit function of the post-dated cheque, keep endorsement (including for discounting) and the legal status that allows shortened enforcement; end technical bounces, physical presentment and slow settlement. In 2021 cheque payments totalled NIS 812 billion, against NIS 382 billion by cards, and more than NIS 120 billion passed through the cheque-discounting industry.

**Compared with our bills:** the consultation describes a bank-centred instrument tied to an account. Our bills meet several of its aims by construction: an accepted bill cannot bounce for a technical reason, since acceptance is recorded before the bill circulates; it is transferred without physical presentment; and every transfer is recorded on the register. Endorsement is replaced by transfer of the bearer bill. One aim is not met: our bills are payable at sight, so they do not reproduce the credit that a post-dated cheque gives the drawer.

[Bank Leumi: digital cheque (January 2016)](https://marketscreener.com/quote/stock/BANK-LEUMI-LE-ISRAEL-BM-6491695/news/Bank-Leumi-Le-Israel-BM-The-End-of-Paper-Cheques-Approaches-Leumi-Launches-the-Digital-Cheque-21746024) — A bank product for issuing cheques digitally within the bank's own channels.
